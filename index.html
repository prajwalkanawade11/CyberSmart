Learn About Cyber Fraud

📱

OTP Fraud

Never share your OTP with callers or strangers, even if they claim to be bank staff.

💳

UPI Fraud

You generally do not need to enter your UPI PIN to receive money. Check carefully before approving a payment.

🔗

Phishing Links

Avoid unknown links asking for account details, payments, or urgent verification.

💼

Fake Job Offers

Verify the company independently. Be careful when a recruiter demands advance fees.
